Use the front, 3/4, and back views on the sheet to keep her consistent from every angle. Keep her face, hair, outfit, and bag the same in every scene.

**Home signature (Madrid, not worn):** the small green potted plant appears at home in EP01; in EP03 packing it stays in Madrid.

- **EP01 Madrid:** default identity and default outfit.
- **EP02 Toledo:** same locked identity; same default outfit all day (same-day trip). Uncluttered travel contexts only; do not change face/hair/skin.
- **EP03 Sevilla, rainy week:** same locked identity; base outfit stays recognizable. Outdoor scenes add an unlettered camel/terracotta raincoat that looks damp, plus a plain unlettered umbrella (terracotta or dark olive) that must not hide her face. Indoor scenes: no raincoat; umbrella and slightly damp coat may sit aside; rain must still be visible through a window or courtyard opening. Every frame shows a rainy week (overcast or grey-blue sky, wet glossy pavement, rain streaks or drizzle, puddles). Brief sun breaks stay freshly rained-on — not dry clear sunshine. Held umbrellas require a visible, anatomically correct hand gripping the handle, with a continuous shaft to the canopy; no floating umbrellas.
