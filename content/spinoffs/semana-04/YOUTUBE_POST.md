Marina goes to her first Spanish cooking class, and now it's time to talk! In these four slow, clear A1–A2 conversations she arrives and meets the teacher, chats with her new classmate Laura while the potatoes cook, flips her first tortilla de patatas (¡menudo lío!), and tastes it at the long table with her new friends.

Every conversation comes from this week's story, *Mi primera clase de cocina española*, and uses its three expressions from Spain: *¡vale!*, *¡menudo lío!* and *¡está de muerte!* This is Spanish from Spain, spoken slowly, in two rounds: first listen and read each line in Spanish with an English translation, then listen again and repeat each line in the pause. After each conversation, we review the key phrases.
