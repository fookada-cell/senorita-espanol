It rains all week in Seville, and Elena keeps talking! In these four slow, clear A1–A2 conversations she shelters from the pouring rain under the arches and chats with a friendly local, buys a museum ticket on a wet morning, enjoys a hot coffee in a patio while the rain falls on the orange tree, and finally sees the sun by the river, where she asks someone to take her photo with the Torre del Oro.

Every conversation comes from this week's story, *Mi semana de lluvia en Sevilla*, and uses its three expressions from Spain: *está lloviendo a cántaros*, *¡qué guay!* and *¡madre mía!* This is Spanish from Spain, spoken slowly, in two rounds: first listen and read each line in Spanish with an English translation, then listen again and repeat each line in the pause. After each conversation, we review the key phrases.
