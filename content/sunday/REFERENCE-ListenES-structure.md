# Reference: ListenES main-video structure vs our Sunday format

Studied (user's transcripts + vidIQ screenshots, 2026-11-24): *El amor me encuentra* (59.4K views), *Me rindo* (52.9K, 31:40), *Ayer, hoy, mañana* (74K in 28 days, 2× outlier, 25:25).

## Their structure
1. Intro (~18 s, English): explains the 3 steps; a 3-step graphic (1 Listen with subtitles · 2 Listen with translation · 3 Listen without subtitles).
2. Listen 1: the story TITLE is read first, then the story; Spanish subtitle in a black bar at the top.
3. Transition: the 3-step graphic again, step 2 highlighted. "Try to listen and repeat after the presenter."
4. Listen 2: Spanish line → ~3–5 s silent repeat gap → English line → short gap. English subtitle in a black bar at the bottom.
5. Transition: graphic, step 3 highlighted. "Listen again… and answer the questions."
6. Listen 3: no subtitles.
7. "Three simple questions… try to answer in the comments." Questions are open (¿Por qué…?, ¿Qué…?, ¿Dónde…?).
8. Vocabulary: 30–45 chunks in story order (phrases, not just nouns), Spanish → pause → English. Works as a story recap.
9. Outro: PDF learning guide (bilingual text, exercises, tests, vocab list), paid link in description, free for channel members.
10. Stories: emotional arc + twist/punchline that repeats the title ("Me rindo", "A veces el amor te encuentra primero"); some built around grammar (past/present/future).
11. Titles: hook + "LEARN SPANISH with Short Story – <Spanish title> (A1-A2)".

## Gaps in our EP01–EP08 format
| Item | Theirs | Ours | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repeat gap in Listen 2 | ~3–5 s after each Spanish line | 0.4 s | HIGH |
| "Listen and repeat" instruction | yes | no | HIGH |
| 3-step roadmap graphic at intro + each transition | yes | no (spoken only) | MEDIUM |
| Story title read at start | yes | no | LOW |
| Questions → "answer in the comments" | yes | timed pause, separate comment question | MEDIUM |
| Vocab size / format | 30–45 phrases, story order, repeat pause | 16 words, 0.3 s gap | MEDIUM |
| PDF guide / membership offer | yes | none | business decision |
| Twist / punchline ending, title = punchline | yes | gentle slice-of-life | content choice |
| Grammar-theme stories | sometimes (tenses) | mostly present tense | idea for future |

## What we have that they don't
Expressions from Spain (with story examples) · Spain-only Spanish · chapters · DELE A2 word list · conversation spin-offs.
